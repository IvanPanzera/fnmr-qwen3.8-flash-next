What does the filter guarantee?

One common layer–expert mask is checked against original traces of the selected phase. In Domain mean it covers all 150 questions; in the other views it covers the selected question. Automatic mode chooses the largest common threshold meeting the loss limit in every layer. Equal scores stay together. The filter changes the display only: it does not change inference, recompute the underlying means or renormalize remaining weights.

How to read this collection

Token matrix: effective normalized weights of the ten experts selected in each layer when the target processes that token. The other 502 entries are zero. This is post-selection routing, not all pre-selection logits or a map of every neuron.

Prefill includes the chat template and special tokens. Rejected speculative drafts are excluded from these committed traces and means.

Question mean: the sum of token matrices in a phase divided by the phase's token count.

Equal weight per question: the mean of question means. Equal weight per token: the sum of all token matrices divided by the total token count.

Final token: where necessary, a final diagnostic forward pass captures the emitted token. It produces no second answer and its time is excluded from prefill/generation speeds.

Timing: the four speed cards always refer to the selected question. Speculative tokens may arrive together; the arrival interval is not an individual token's computation time.

Corpus: synthetic tasks, 20 families per domain, collected as a balanced subset of 1,800 out of 3,600 planned variants. 1,435 train and 365 holdout questions; entire task families were held out. 97 outputs reached the 384-token generation cap. Answer accuracy was not scored.

Translations: English questions and automatically translated answers are reading aids created after collection. Original code, formulas and structured data remain literal. Traces and token selection always refer to the original text.
